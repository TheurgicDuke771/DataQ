### 2. A release-tag publish can now be silently cancelled by a merge — real defect (latent)
`.github/workflows/publish-images.yml:66-68`

`main` pushes now enter the same `group: publish-images` as `v*` tag pushes. ### 5. `AzureKeyVaultStore.close()` doesn't release the credential's sockets — real defect, undercuts the fix's own goal
`backend/app/core/secrets.py:243-253`, credential built at `:192`

`SecretClient.close()` is `self._client.close()` — it closes the KV client's pipeline transport only. `DefaultAzureCredential` is constructed inline at line 192, never retained, and owns a **separate** transport session per chained credential (`ChainedTokenCredential.close`: *"Close the transport session of each credential in the chain"*). ### 6. `close()` on an injected-client store detaches it and silently upgrades the store to real network I/O
