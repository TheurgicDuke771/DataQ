**A contract export no longer shows the listed values of a column marked sensitive.** A data
contract can list a column's values: a property's `enum` and `examples`, a rule's `validValues`
or `missingValues`, a custom rule's `value_set`. When that column is classified as sensitive
after the contract was imported (the suite's column policy, a warehouse tag, fail-closed mode or
the PII classifier), the exported ODCS document now shows `<redacted>` in their place, on the
REST export and the MCP `export_contract` tool, for every reader. The document names what it
withheld: `dataq.valuesWithheld` on the property or rule and `dataq.redactedColumns` on the
document, and the MCP tool also returns `redacted_columns` and `redaction_reasons`. The stored
contract and its checks keep the real values. Importing a document that still holds `<redacted>`
in one of those places is refused (`422 value_set_redacted`), so an export can no longer
overwrite a contract's real values or switch off the checks they back.
