**A declared seasonal calendar for anomaly checks.** Workspace admins keep one calendar of named seasonal periods (recurring month-and-day ranges, or dated entries grouped into a series across years) through the REST API, audited like other admin changes. A per-period anomaly check with `yearly` on attaches the entries that apply to its table: a day inside one is compared with the same day of the period's previous occurrence, so a sale that moves or a fixed date lines up, and the days after it are not judged against it. In a period's first year a rise is reported as within the period rather than banded, while a drop still alerts. Every result says which entry applied and what the run would have scored without the calendar.
